# Pyre Licensing Policy

## Current model

Ginkgo Pyre is an official proprietary commercial edition of Ginkgo Tree.

New proprietary revisions from August 1, 2026 are intended to be distributed only under written commercial terms from Ginkgo AI Systems, except for files or components that carry a separate open-source or third-party license.

## Historical MIT releases

This repository previously used the MIT License. The historical license text is preserved at `LICENSES/MIT-2025.txt`.

Changing the top-level license does not erase rights previously granted for copies and versions distributed under MIT. Historical versions and qualifying historical materials remain subject to the license that accompanied them.

## Scope of the commercial notice

The commercial notice applies prospectively to materials that:

- are added or materially revised after the transition;
- are owned by Ginkgo AI Systems or validly assigned/licensed to it;
- are not separately marked under another license.

Repository history must be consulted when determining the license of an older file or revision.

## Contributions

Do not accept external code into proprietary revisions without confirming contribution rights. A Contributor License Agreement, assignment or employer authorization may be required.

## Third-party components

Dependencies, copied components and generated assets retain their own licenses and notices. Do not remove upstream attribution.

## Legal review

This document records the intended operational boundary. A qualified lawyer should review the transition, contributor history and commercial agreements before broad distribution, source licensing, OEM licensing or material transactions.
